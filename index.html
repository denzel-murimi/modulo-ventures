What We Do

Our Capabilities

Computing Hardware

Networking & Infrastructure

About Us

Kenya's Trusted ICT Supply Partner

Modulo Ventures Limited was established to close the reliability gap in Kenya's ICT supply chain. Too many institutions suffer delayed deliveries, counterfeit hardware, and non-compliant documentation. We built our business around fixing exactly that.

Operating from Nairobi with a nationwide logistics network, our team manages every step from quotation to post-delivery support — giving procurement officers the certainty they need. Under the leadership of our directors, Peter Keith Karanja, Denzel Murimi Kahuthia, and Khachina Mweha, we navigate the complexities of the electronics supply chain with a rigorous focus on compliance, quality assurance, and seamless execution.
