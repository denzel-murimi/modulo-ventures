    How an order runs

    The same four steps whether it's one printer or a full lab.

    1. Quote

      Send your list, RFQ or tender. You get an itemised quotation within 4 business hours.

    2. LPO

      Once your LPO arrives we order from authorised distributors and check every serial number with the manufacturer.

    3. Deliver and install

      We deliver with a delivery note and serial list, then install and test on site.

    4. Invoice and support

      You get an eTIMS invoice and a completion certificate to sign. We stay on call for warranty and support.
